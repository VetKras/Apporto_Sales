Authority levels

| Level | Label | Meaning | Conflict behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Low / new or unknown employee | Useful field signal, not trusted to change source truth | Block conflicting update immediately. Explain the conflict and tell the user to have their supervisor add or approve it. |
| 2 | Standard sales/product user | Trusted for deal feedback and customer notes | Create proposed update for review. Do not directly overwrite source truth. |
| 3 | Manager / domain owner | Trusted subject-matter owner for their domain | Perform deeper analysis and create a strong proposed update. May update only where product rules allow and audit trail is preserved. |
| 4 | Executive / company leadership | Highest-trust source for business direction | Perform deferential conflict analysis, ask confirmation on meaningful conflicts, then apply audited override after confirmation. |

No silent overwrites
High authority does not mean automatic overwrite. It means Portia treats the source as more likely to be correct, analyzes conflicts more deeply, asks confirmation when needed, and records a durable audit trail.
