PRODUCT: CoTutor
==================================================

id: seed-product-cotutor
slug: cotutor
name: CoTutor
category: AI learning and authoring assistant
status: active

Positioning
CoTutor is an assignment-aware, rubric-aligned, faculty-guardrailed AI learning and authoring assistant that supports students while they work.

What it is
CoTutor is not a general-purpose chatbot. It is a governed AI learning companion embedded in student workflows and scoped to assignment instructions, rubrics, and faculty-defined guardrails.

Primary users
- Students receiving guided support while writing or completing coursework
- Faculty configuring assistance levels and teaching method
- Teaching teams managing course-level AI behavior

Buyer
Institutions seeking responsible, governed AI adoption for learning support.

Core capabilities
- Assignment-aware AI assistance
- Rubric-aligned guidance
- Faculty-controlled guardrails
- Configurable assistance levels
- Teaching-method control such as Socratic, problem-based, or explanatory support
- LMS integration
- Microsoft Word add-in
- Google Docs integration
- Pre-submission review using PowerGrader review logic

Relationship to other AI Suite products
- Uses PowerGrader as the pre-submission review engine.
- Reduces integrity risk upstream before TrustEd review is needed.
- Complements ExamSpace by supporting learning outside controlled exam environments.

Sales-safe claims
- Assignment-aware, not generic AI chat.
- Faculty decides how AI helps.
- Designed for governed institutional AI adoption.
- Supports learning, revision, and reasoning rather than default full-answer generation.

Known risks / data gaps
- Guardrail misconfiguration can create over-assistance.
- Student expectations may be shaped by unrestricted consumer AI tools.
- Google Docs integration is noted as hardening/in progress in product context.
- Claims about measured outcomes should cite a specific pilot/source before customer use.

PRODUCT: PowerGrader
==================================================

id: seed-product-powergrader
slug: powergrader
name: PowerGrader
category: AI-assisted grading engine
status: active

Positioning
PowerGrader is an AI-assisted grading engine that helps faculty grade faster while preserving instructor control, rubric fidelity, and academic judgment.

What it is
PowerGrader is an LMS-native grading workspace. It produces rubric-aligned draft scores and feedback for instructor review. It does not automate final grading decisions.

Primary users
- Faculty and instructors grading assignments at scale
- Teaching assistants and instructional teams
- Academic leadership monitoring grading consistency and efficiency

Buyer
Institutions seeking scalable grading support with clear AI governance.

Core capabilities
- Rubric-aligned draft grading
- Criterion-level feedback
- Instructor review/edit/approve workflow
- Draft and publish states
- LMS course, assignment, roster, and submission sync
- Grade passback
- Multi-format submission support
- Direct escalation into TrustEd evidence views when integrity review is needed

Relationship to other AI Suite products
- Provides the review and feedback engine for CoTutor pre-submission review.
- Integrates with TrustEd for integrity evidence review.
- Complements ExamSpace by handling assessment grading after controlled exams.

Sales-safe claims
- AI-assisted grading that respects faculty control.
- Faculty remains final authority.
- Rubric-first workflow supports transparency and consistency.
- Designed as decision support, not automated assessment.

Known risks / data gaps
- Canvas is operational; D2L and Moodle stabilization are noted as active/in progress.
- Grading quality depends on rubric completeness and assignment structure.
- Avoid absolute claims such as "eliminates grading time" unless a specific customer/pilot source supports it.

PRODUCT: TrustEd
==================================================

id: seed-product-trusted
slug: trusted
name: TrustEd
category: Academic integrity and authorship evidence
status: active

Positioning
TrustEd is an authorship and academic integrity evidence platform that helps faculty understand how student work was created without making automatic accusations.

What it is
TrustEd analyzes writing process and behavioral signals to surface explainable authorship evidence for faculty review. It supports human judgment rather than replacing institutional policy.

Primary users
- Faculty evaluating writing authenticity
- Instructors reviewing flagged submissions
- Academic leadership
- Integrity and compliance teams

Buyer
Institutions seeking transparent, defensible academic integrity tooling for the AI era.

Core capabilities
- Keystroke/process playback
- Writing speed and revision pattern analysis
- Copy/paste detection and classification
- Writing style consistency analysis
- Outlier and anomaly detection
- Integrity evidence reports
- Faculty review workflow

Relationship to other AI Suite products
- CoTutor supports responsible AI use upstream.
- TrustEd verifies authenticity downstream.
- PowerGrader can pivot into TrustEd evidence review.
- ExamSpace handles controlled assessments; TrustEd focuses on authorship/process evidence.

Sales-safe claims
- Behavior-based and evidence-focused.
- Designed for transparency, not black-box accusation.
- Faculty makes all final determinations.
- Helps reduce over-reliance on text-only AI detection.

Known risks / data gaps
- Faculty may misinterpret indicators as definitive judgments.
- Must avoid language that implies automatic misconduct detection or penalties.
- Customer-facing claims should emphasize evidence and review, not accusation.

PRODUCT: ExamSpace
==================================================

id: seed-product-examspace
slug: examspace
name: ExamSpace
category: Controlled VDI assessment environment
status: active

Positioning
ExamSpace is a VDI-based controlled assessment environment that enforces application, system, and network policy at the infrastructure level.

What it is
ExamSpace delivers cloud-hosted virtual exam desktops through Apporto infrastructure. It is not browser-only lockdown and not AI proctoring. It supports restricted exams and tool-enabled assessments.

Primary users
- Students taking high-stakes or software-dependent assessments
- Faculty administering exams
- IT teams managing assessment environments
- Academic leadership overseeing assessment integrity and equity

Buyer
Institutions seeking secure, flexible alternatives to browser lockdown tools and local lab constraints.

Core capabilities
- Full virtual desktop exam environment
- Infrastructure-level application control
- Admin-managed base exam desktops
- Faculty clone/configure workflow
- URL/network enforcement
- LMS launch using LTI 1.3
- Access to advanced software when allowed
- Ability to disable, limit, or explicitly allow AI tools according to assessment design

Relationship to other AI Suite products
- Can enable or disable CoTutor according to assessment policy.
- Provides controlled conditions before PowerGrader handles grading.
- Complements TrustEd, which focuses on authorship/process evidence rather than environment enforcement.

Sales-safe claims
- Controls the full assessment environment, not just the browser.
- Supports both restricted and authentic tool-enabled assessments.
- Helps provide equitable access to required software through cloud desktops.
- Enforces policy centrally at the virtual desktop level.

Known risks / data gaps
- Faculty misconfiguration of allowed tools can weaken assessment design.
- Peak exam periods can increase support load.
- High-concurrency reliability must be validated for large deployments.
- Do not describe ExamSpace as AI proctoring or automated surveillance.
